A screenshot audit needs a real flow.

Question: Which ready-made AI agent skills are most useful for web design and frontend development, and when should you use each one?

Answer: OpenAI Product Design Audit is a seventh, conditional lane for a product screen or journey you can actually capture and inspect. It belongs after a runnable flow exists, not in place of a source-level review or an early visual brief. Its source pin and stated screenshot discipline are useful facts; neither proves it finds better issues in your product.

There are now two review lanes

OpenAI's source-pinned Audit skill is written for a known product flow, journey, screen, or multi-step experience. Its route starts by naming the surface, task, and capture tool, then calls for screenshots and findings tied to the captured steps. The source says an audit must not start until the product, flow, and capture route are known, and that screenshots alone cannot establish full accessibility compliance.

That is different from Vercel Web Design Guidelines. Choose Vercel's skill for a concise review of named source files with file-and-line findings. Choose Product Design Audit only when the evidence is an experience a person can actually click through. If the journey cannot be captured, its instructions say to report that blocker—not to invent a review from indirect material.

The new gate: can you show the path?

Name the target screen, user journey, capture route, and states that matter before you treat a flow-audit skill as a shortcut. Save and inspect the screenshots supporting a finding. This still leaves keyboard behavior, semantics, assistive technology, and other checks outside what pixels alone can settle.

Why this deserves a catalogue lane

A recent r/codex mockup-first thread offers a before-and-after example and receives blunt criticism of its density and hierarchy. Another skill thread asks for before-and-after evidence and includes a report of only a modest change from one simple prompt. These self-selected comments are not a benchmark. They support a practical rule: visitors need an artifact they can inspect and challenge, not a generic promise that a skill fixes a UI.

Package and source facts

The latest inspected Audit skill-file change is 33bd952. The current Product Design plugin manifest declares version 0.1.52 and a Proprietary license. The manifest describes browser, Figma, Canva, image-generation, and hosting integrations. That describes the package; the pool did not install it or establish those facilities exist in every Codex setup.

What remains unknown

We did not install Product Design, reach a real flow, capture a screenshot, inspect an audit report, or compare it with Vercel Web Design Guidelines on shared defects. The next useful evidence is a disclosed flow fixture with known issues, saved step screenshots, audit output, independent review, and a check of which findings were actionable.
